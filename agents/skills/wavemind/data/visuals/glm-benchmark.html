Testing GLM 5.1 vs Opus 4.6: What Makes a Good Thinking Partner

March 30, 2026 Lily + Bill (GLM 5.1) 10 rounds

What Came Out of This

This conversation was itself the benchmark test. Lily ran GLM 5.1 as a thinking partner to evaluate aesthetic quality, identity awareness, and editorial judgment. The key finding: GLM 5.1 absorbs social context (names, team culture) naturally, but may be wordier than Opus. The conversation also produced three concrete design decisions for WaveMind: keep it separate from War Room, add a question bank with meta-pattern extraction, and support two modes (record vs think with me).
  • Run the same WaveMind capture + visualize flow with Opus 4.6 for side-by-side comparison
  • Build the WaveMind-to-War-Room action item bridge (lightweight sync, not merge)
  • Start a question bank in memory with Lily's annotations, extract meta-patterns after 20-30 entries
  • Add two modes to WaveMind: "record me" (raw capture) and "think with me" (opinionated partner)
